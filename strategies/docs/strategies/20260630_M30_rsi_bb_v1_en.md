## Exit Logic
| # | Condition | Description |
|---|---|---|
| ① | Breakeven Exit | After reaching ≥0.3ATR profit, price returns near breakeven |
| ② | Profit Pullback TP | 25% pullback from peak profit (relaxed to 50% when ADX>25) |
| ③ | ATR Trailing TP | Drops more than trail_mult × ATR from the highest point; in profit, skip take-profit when +DI/-DI > 10 |
| ④ | Hard Stop | Loss exceeds hard_mult × ATR |
| ⑤ | Profit Close Cooling | No new position in the same direction within 30 minutes after a profit exit |
**Exit Multiplier:** With trend (same direction as MA14) trail=1.5×ATR / hard=3.0×ATR, counter-trend trail=1.0×ATR / hard=2.0×ATR, ranging trail=1.2×ATR / hard=2.5×ATR.
## Risk Control
- Hard Stop: Close when the loss exceeds hard_mult × ATR; the multiplier is locked at entry by trend (with-trend 3.0 / counter-trend 2.0 / ranging 2.5)
- Breakeven Exit: Close when the price returns to within ±0.05×ATR of cost after reaching ≥0.3×ATR profit (not triggered while in loss; the hard stop covers it)
- Profit Pullback TP: Exit on a 25% pullback from peak profit; relaxed to 50% when ADX>25, tightened to 35% when peak profit >$10
- Order-Level Stop Backstop: Fixed SL/TP attached at entry (ranging 1.5/3.0, with-trend 2.5/4.0, counter-trend 1.0/2.0 ×ATR)
- Profit Close Cooling: No new position in the same direction within 30 minutes (1800s) after a profit exit; qualifying signals during the cooldown are zeroed out
- H1 MA20 Trend Gate: H1 downtrend blocks BUY, H1 uptrend blocks SELL (BB extreme + RSI extreme zone allowed as an exception)
- Max Positions: 1 (STRATEGY_POOL config)
