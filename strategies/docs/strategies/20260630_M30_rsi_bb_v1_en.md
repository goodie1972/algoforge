## Exit Logic
| # | Condition | Description |
|---|---|---|
| ① | Breakeven Exit | After reaching ≥0.3ATR profit, price returns near breakeven |
| ② | Profit Pullback TP | 25% pullback from peak profit (relaxed to 50% when ADX>25) |
| ③ | ATR Trailing TP | Drops more than trail_mult × ATR from the highest point; in profit, skip take-profit when +DI/-DI > 10 |
| ④ | Hard Stop | Loss exceeds hard_mult × ATR |
| ⑤ | Profit Close Cooling | No new position in the same direction within 30 minutes after a profit exit |
**Exit Multiplier:** With trend (same direction as MA14) trail=1.5×ATR / hard=3.0×ATR, counter-trend trail=1.0×ATR / hard=2.0×ATR, ranging trail=1.2×ATR / hard=2.5×ATR.
