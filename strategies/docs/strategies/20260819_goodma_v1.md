## 入场逻辑

### 做多

| # | 条件 | 说明 |
|:-:|:----|:----|
| 1 | 价格回踩 ≤ 60SMA | 价格从上方回踩至 SMA60 附近 |
| 2 | 60SMA 方向向上 | 均线呈多头排列 |

### Long Entry

| # | Condition | Description |
|:-:|:---------|:------------|
| 1 | Price pullback ≤ 60SMA | Price pulls back to SMA60 from above |
| 2 | 60SMA direction up | MA bullish alignment |

### 做空

| # | 条件 | 说明 |
|:-:|:----|:----|
| 1 | 价格反弹 ≥ 60SMA | 价格从下方反弹至 SMA60 附近 |
| 2 | 60SMA 方向向下 | 均线呈空头排列 |

### Short Entry

| # | Condition | Description |
|:-:|:---------|:------------|
| 1 | Price bounce ≥ 60SMA | Price bounces to SMA60 from below |
| 2 | 60SMA direction down | MA bearish alignment |

## 出场逻辑

| # | 条件 | 说明 |
|:-:|:----|:----|
| ① | EMA20 方向反转 | 多单：EMA20 向下；空单：EMA20 向上 |
| ② | ATR 硬止损 | 2.0×ATR 亏损止损 |

## Exit Logic

| # | Condition | Description |
|:-:|:---------|:------------|
| ① | EMA20 direction reversal | Long: EMA20 down; Short: EMA20 up |
| ② | ATR Hard Stop | 2.0×ATR loss stop |
