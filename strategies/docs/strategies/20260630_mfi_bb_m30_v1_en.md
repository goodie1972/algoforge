**Timeframe:** M30

## Entry Logic
### Long Entry (Oversold)
| # | Condition | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | MFI < 20 | Money flow extremely weak |
| 2 | close ≤ BB lower band | Price breaks below the lower band |
| 3 | BB bandwidth expansion | Volatility amplified |
### Short Entry (Overbought)
| # | Condition | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | MFI > 80 | Money flow extremely strong |
| 2 | close ≥ BB upper band | Price breaks above the upper band |
| 3 | BB bandwidth expansion | Volatility amplified |
## Exit Logic
| # | Condition | Description |
|---|---|---|
| ① | MFI direction reversal | Bidirectional hedging lock exit |
## Risk Control
- No traditional stop loss / take profit; all exits are managed by check_ema20_exit; order-level backstop stop = entry price ±5% (BUY attaches 0.95×entry, SELL attaches 1.05×entry)
- With-Trend Exit: On an opposite entry signal (the other MFI extreme + touching the other band), lock/exit
- Counter-Trend Exit Target: Price returns to the BB middle band, or moves half of the BB bandwidth at entry
- Max Positions: 1 (STRATEGY_POOL config)
## Parameter Reference
| Parameter | Value | Description |
|---|---|---|
| mfi_overbought | 80 | MFI overbought threshold (short condition) |
| mfi_oversold | 20 | MFI oversold threshold (long condition) |
| bb_period / bb_std | 20 / 2.0 | Bollinger Bands period and standard deviation multiplier |
| tolerance_bars | 3 | Tolerance bars for band touch |
